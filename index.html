    OpenEvo

    OpenEvo is a low-cost, fully open-source turbidostat for continuous culture and directed evolution. A browser interface (or the stand-alone LCD menu) runs it from an Arduino Mega 2560. It monitors optical density in real time, swaps in fresh media at a set OD threshold, controls temperature and stirring, and drives programmable LEDs as a selection stimulus.

    The first unit costs about $300 in parts, and a second about $200. The authors report that an undergraduate with no electronics background built a working unit with minimal supervision.

    About this model

    All 26 printed modules are the team's actual STL files, placed using their real geometry: the 5 mm rod holes, matching screw patterns and stacking bosses. The step sequence, part list, pin assignments and wire colours come from the Type D Mk4 assembly manual, the bill of materials and the wiring checklist. Electronic boards, pumps and glassware are approximate procedural models.
