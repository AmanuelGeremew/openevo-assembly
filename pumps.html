    Replacing the pumps

    The OpenEVO Type D Mk4 Assembly Manual has no procedure for replacing a pump on a finished machine. This page undoes and repeats the manual’s pump steps (Hardware Mounting 29, 30 and 69; Function Testing 1–5) on the machine as it stands at the end of the 3D Assembly Manual, with the moulds and other accessories put away. The firmware’s CALIBRATE PUMP menu takes the place of Function Testing 6–7.

    Steps from the manual show its text word for word. Everything else is marked “Not in the manual”. That covers whole steps (powering off, taking the tubing and wires off, lifting the pump out, seating the new pump, saving the calibration) and short notes inside the manual’s steps. Notes on the firmware come from easyEVOTypeD.ino and CONFIG.TXT in the repository; LCD screens use the firmware’s own strings.

    Differences from the 3D Assembly Manual model:

    • From the step 69 photo: each pump’s two wires sit in that pump’s own pair of Motor Shield terminals, with the GND hole between the M1 and M2 pairs (and between M3 and M4) empty.
    • From the Function Testing step 1 photo: the 60mm tube segments rise straight up from the pump nozzles, with the luer adapter’s barb in the tube.
    • Not in the manual: pump M2’s two segments (and M3’s, slightly) bend a few millimetres sideways at the nozzle, clear of the wires that drop between Modules C and G.
    • Not in the manual: the four braided pump wire pairs arch over the motherboard at slightly different heights, so they don’t pass through each other.

    The screwdriver is the only tool shown. Soldering is shown as the solder glowing, as in the 3D Assembly Manual.
